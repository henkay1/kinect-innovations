Cookie Notice

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

This Cookie Notice explains how Kinect Innovations LLC ("we," "us," "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, and how you can manage your preferences. It should be read together with our Privacy Policy.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. We also use similar technologies such as local storage, which our website uses to remember items in your shopping cart.

Categories of Cookies We Use

Category Purpose Consent Required?
Strictly Necessary Enable core functionality such as remembering items in your cart and recording your cookie preference. No — required for the site to function
Functional Remember preferences to improve your experience across visits. Yes
Analytics Help us understand how visitors use our website so we can improve it (used only if enabled by the site operator). Yes

Your Choices

When you first visit our website, a cookie banner allows you to Accept All or Reject Non-Essential cookies. You can change your preference at any time by clearing your browser's local storage for this site, or by adjusting your browser's cookie settings. Strictly necessary cookies cannot be disabled, as they are required for basic site functions like your shopping cart.

Third-Party Cookies

Our payment processor (PayPal) may set its own cookies when you reach the checkout page, governed by their respective privacy and cookie policies.

Browser Controls

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies through their settings. Please note that blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect the functionality of our website, including the shopping cart.

Contact Us

Questions about this Cookie Notice can be directed to support@kinectinnovationsllc.com.
