About Kinect Innovations LLC

Environment management, treated as one discipline.

We started Kinect Innovations LLC around a simple observation: the crew that organizes and cleans a space and the crew that wires and maintains its technology are almost always separate — and the handoff between them is where problems slip through. We built one team to do both.

OUR APPROACH

One visit, both domains

Every technician we send is trained to consider physical workspace and technical infrastructure together — because a decluttered desk with tangled cabling behind it, or a clean server closet in a disorganized room, is only half the job.

OUR STANDARD

Documented, not just done

Infrastructure audits, network changes, and automation setups come with a written record of what we found and what we touched — so you always know the current state of your space and systems.

OUR PRODUCTS

Fragrance-free by default

We use fragrance-free cleaning and disinfecting products as standard, since fragrance sensitivities and allergies are common — scented options are available on request.
